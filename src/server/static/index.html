Computational Scaling Laws: CNN vs. Transformer

How computational complexity shifts as audio duration grows from 1 second to 60 seconds:

Audio Length Frames GFLOPs CNN Share O(T) Attn Share O(T²)
The Scaling Takeaway: • For short audio (< 10s): The 7-layer 1D CNN accounts for >92% of compute (convolutional filterbanks).
• For long audio (1–10 minutes): The 320x downsampling keeps attention manageable, but the $O(T^2)$ self-attention stage expands rapidly, increasing its share from 6% to over 16%!
